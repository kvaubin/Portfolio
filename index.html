La Gang du Show

Podcast, production,
and everything after.

RoleProducer · Editor
PlatformInstagram — @lagangdushow
FootageShot by @jemcee

Every episode starts as raw camera angles and mic tracks, shot and recorded by @jemcee. From there, it's mine — I produce the structure, edit the full episode, cut it down into clips, and run the posting schedule that gets it in front of people.

Production Details

What I Handle

Full-episode editing, short-form clip cuts, captioning, posting schedule, and the overall creative direction of how the show shows up online — the pacing, the pull-quotes, the moments that get pulled out for social.

Logo & Graphic Design

Beyond editing, I designed the show's current logo and the graphics used across sponsorships and cross-promotion — plus a short animated logo reveal used as a video opener.
